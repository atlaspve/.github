# AtlasPVE

**The safe operations layer for Proxmox.**

AtlasPVE makes your Proxmox VE infrastructure local, visible, auditable and safely manageable. It runs on your own Proxmox host as a local agent. Nothing connects in from outside, and the product works without an internet connection.

### What Atlas does

- Brings your hosts, virtual machines and containers into one clear operational view.
- Surfaces Proxmox best-practice checks: protection locks, snapshots, updates, storage health.
- Makes risky actions deliberate: guarded updates, a snapshot before changes, a clear warning before any destructive step.
- Keeps Proxmox as Proxmox. It adds a layer of safety and clarity, it does not replace the platform.

### Auditable by design

The agent that runs on your host is AGPL licensed and installs as readable source you can inspect on your own machine. The product's analysis engine and interface are proprietary.
